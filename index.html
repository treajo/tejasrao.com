Case study

Building Twist's AI support agent from nothing

Fin on Intercom, live since June 2026. Nobody asked for it. I proposed it, won support and sales leadership over, and built it.

Twist sells synthetic DNA to researchers. They write in about everything from a shipping date to a gene fragment that would not assemble, and a confident wrong answer can cost someone a month of lab work. The routine questions were eating the team's week, but a guessing agent would have cost more trust than it saved time.

How I got it to 60 percent

  • Read months of past conversations and sorted them into safe to automate, handle with care, and always a scientist.
  • Turned scattered scientific documentation into a structured, retrieval-ready knowledge base, so every answer has one source behind it.
  • Designed the guardrails that decide what the agent answers and what it routes, and built context-aware handoff that sends each one to sales, customer support or technical support.
  • Reviewed conversations every week, tagged each miss by cause, and fixed the cause rather than the symptom. That is what moved resolution from 0 to 60 percent in five months, with satisfaction holding at 93.

From answering to doing

  • Extended the agent past questions into tasks, using MCP integrations across Intercom, JIRA, ServiceNow and Salesforce.
  • Order cancellations that used to mean manual updates in three systems now run end to end, as do account manager lookups.

What stuck

  • Most of what looked like model failure was missing or messy content.
  • A clean handoff is a feature. People trust an agent that knows where its knowledge ends.
It got better every time I fixed a document, not a prompt.

Twist gave this work the Innovation in Global Support Award in 2026.
